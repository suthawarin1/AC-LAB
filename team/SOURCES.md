# Team data and assets

Seed names, Thai spelling, academic titles and expertise came primarily from the user's supplied legacy AC Lab HTML and its team roster. PI grouping, the three Researcher slots, and the three Student nicknames follow the user's instructions in this conversation. Grouping is editable and does not imply an employment relationship with KKU for every collaborator.

The six Researcher/Student entries contain nicknames or an explicitly labeled empty slot only. No surnames, academic qualifications, expertise, individual email addresses or institutional affiliations were invented for them.

Existing titles were retained from the supplied roster except where a clearly identified primary directory supported the displayed title. Professional contact details should be reviewed by the lab before publication; the Manager supports direct updates. A missing individual email uses the lab contact link, without presenting it as that person's email.

## Fonts

The standalone pages embed the same Anuphan and Noto Serif Thai font subsets as the approved main site. SIL Open Font License notices are included in each HTML and the existing `about/fonts/` directory. DM Sans and Fraunces may load from Google Fonts; local fallback fonts remain available without that connection.
