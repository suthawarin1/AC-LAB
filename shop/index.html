About us

Research you can hold in your hand

AC Lab X is the retail identity that carries science from the bench at Khon Kaen University to registered, everyday products — built and operated by Sciseelt Co., Ltd.

AC Lab — the research

Since 2010, the AC Lab at the Faculty of Science, Khon Kaen University has sequenced plant genomes, profiled medicinal-plant chemistry, and tested plant compounds on human cells, led by Prof. Dr. Arunrat Chaveerach.
