Principal investigator

Prof. Dr. Arunrat Chaveerach

Professor of Biology · Founder, AC Lab (2010)

Research interests

Plant cytogenetics, genomics and DNA barcoding; the chemistry of medicinal plants; and the biomedical activity of plant-derived compounds against cancer cells.

Current focus

Bridging chloroplast genomics with phytochemistry — linking the evolutionary story of a plant to the molecules it makes and what they do in human cells.
